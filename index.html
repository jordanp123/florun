    About FloRun

    FloRun estimates flow rates from timed volume captures, calibrated 5-gallon bucket readings, triangular V-notch weir charts, and manually entered values.

    Your data

    Everything stays on this device. FloRun makes no network requests, uses no analytics, and works fully offline. The last 25 measurements are kept; saving a 26th removes the oldest.

    !
    Add to Home Screen to keep your data

    Browsers may clear storage for sites that are not installed and go unused for a while. Installing FloRun to your Home Screen — and exporting anything you need to keep — protects your measurements.

    V-notch weir setup

    The weir tables assume a correctly installed weir: sharp crest, free discharge below the notch, head measured three to four times the maximum head upstream, an adequate approach pool, end contractions, and a level crest. If your installation does not meet these conditions, treat readings as rough estimates only.
